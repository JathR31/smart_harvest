1. Getting Started

Creating an account

If you don't have an account yet, open the SmartHarvest login page and click "Register here" to go to the sign-up form.

FieldNotes
Full NameRequired
Email AddressRequired, must not already be registered
Password / Confirm PasswordRequired, at least 8 characters, both must match
Municipality / CityRequired — choose your municipality in Benguet from the dropdown
Mobile NumberOptional — Philippine format, the "+63" is already filled in for you
Terms & Privacy checkboxRequired before you can create your account

Once you click "Create Account", you're signed in immediately and taken straight to your Dashboard.

Logging in

  1. Go to the SmartHarvest login page.
  2. Choose a login method at the top: Email Login or RSBSA Login.
  3. Email Login: enter your email and password, optionally check "Remember me," then click Sign In.
  4. RSBSA Login: enter only your RSBSA number (e.g. 4-11-10-001-00045) — no password needed — then click Sign In.
Tip RSBSA login only works if a DA officer has already registered your RSBSA number in the system. If it doesn't work, use Email Login instead or message your DA officer.

Forgot your password? Click "Forgot password?" on the login page to receive a reset code by email.

2. Dashboard

Your Dashboard is the home screen after logging in. It gives you a quick overview of your farm and the region:

Expected Harvest

An estimate of your upcoming harvest, powered by the system's machine-learning model.

Top Crops

The best-performing crops for your municipality based on historical and current data.

Climate Snapshot

Current climate conditions compared to the historical average for your area.

Optimal Planting

A recommendation for what to plant next and when, based on ML analysis.

The left sidebar is how you move around the whole app — Dashboard, My Crops, Planting Schedule, Yield Analysis, Forecast, Market Prices, Announcements, Inbox, and Settings.

You can also switch your location using the dropdown in the top bar (e.g. "La Trinidad, Benguet") to see data for a different municipality.

3. My Crops

This is where you record and track everything you've planted.

At the top you'll see four summary cards: Total Crops, Total Area (hectares), Expected Yield (metric tons, ML-estimated), and Growing (how many crops are currently active).

Adding a crop

Click "Add Crop" and fill in:

FieldNotes
Crop TypeRequired
VarietyOptional
Planting DateRequired
Expected Harvest DateOptional — the system will estimate it for you if left blank
Area Planted (hectares)Required
MunicipalityRequired
Plot LocationOptional, e.g. "Lot 3, Barangay Wangal"
Seed SourceOptional
NotesOptional

When you save, the button shows "Saving & Predicting..." while the system calculates your expected yield and a confidence percentage.

Managing your crops

Each crop appears as its own card. You can:

  • Update its Status at any time: Planning → Planted → Growing → Harvested (or Failed).
  • See its expected yield, confidence %, planting/harvest dates, and any notes you added.
  • Delete it using the trash icon.
Tip If you haven't added any crops yet, this page will suggest high-potential crops for your area to help you decide what to plant.

4. Weather Forecast

Open Forecast from the sidebar to see:

  • Current Conditions — temperature, "feels like," humidity, and today's rainfall.
  • 7-Day Temperature Forecast — a chart of expected highs and lows for the week.
  • Monthly Rainfall Prediction — a longer-range view to help with planning.

Use the municipality dropdown at the top to check the forecast for a different area.

5. Planting Schedule

This page recommends the best planting windows for different crops, based on machine-learning predictions combined with historical data for your municipality. Each entry shows the crop, recommended planting date, expected harvest date, and an expected yield with confidence level.

6. Yield Analysis

This page shows how your crops (and crops in your area) are performing over time — average yield, best-performing crop, total production, and total area, along with monthly trends and a forecast chart.

7. Market Prices

Found under the Market Prices tab on your Dashboard. Shows current prices per kilogram for different crops, the demand level (e.g. High, Moderate), and whether prices are trending up or down — useful for deciding when and where to sell.

8. Announcements

DA officers post announcements (advisories, weather warnings, market updates, urgent notices) that appear here. Open the Announcements tab to see the full list.

Real-time notifications You don't need to keep checking the Announcements tab — whenever a new announcement is posted, a floating notification card pops up at the top of your screen automatically, no matter which page you're on (Dashboard, Forecast, Yield Analysis, or Planting Schedule). Click it to jump straight to the full announcement, or click the ✕ to dismiss it.

9. Inbox / Messages

Use the Inbox to message your DA officer directly.

  1. Click "New" to start a conversation, add a subject and your message, and send.
  2. Your message can also be sent as an SMS automatically, in case the officer isn't online.
  3. Existing conversations appear in a list on the left; click one to open the full chat thread on the right.
  4. Use the Quick Replies buttons for common responses, or type your own message and press Enter (or click Send) to reply.

New messages arrive automatically while you have the Inbox open, and an unread-message badge appears on the Inbox link in the sidebar whenever you have something new waiting.

10. Chat Assistant

Look for the round chat icon in the bottom-right corner of the screen — it's available on Dashboard, Forecast, Yield Analysis, and Planting Schedule.

This is a quick-answer assistant that responds to common questions typed in your own words, such as:

  • "How do I check the weather forecast?"
  • "Where can I see market prices?"
  • "How do I message a DA officer?"
  • "How do I update my profile?"
  • "How does RSBSA login work?"

If it doesn't recognize your question, it will suggest topics it can help with instead.

11. Settings

Open Settings from the sidebar to view and manage your profile.

  • Personal Information: Full Name, Email, Phone, Location.
  • Farm Information: Farm Name, Farm Size, Primary Crop Types, Years of Experience, and a short bio about your farm.
  • Click "Edit Profile" to update any of these details, then "Save Changes."
  • Notification preferences (weather alerts, planting reminders, weekly yield reports) can be toggled here.
Note The "Change Password" option currently shows a "coming soon" message — it isn't active yet. If you need your password changed, use "Forgot password?" on the login page, or contact your DA officer.

12. Language & Logout

Use the language dropdown in the top bar to switch the interface into Tagalog, Ilocano, or other supported languages.

To sign out, click your profile icon (or the Logout option) in the top-right corner.

1. Getting Started

DA Officers and Admins sign in through a separate, dedicated login page from farmers.

  1. Go to the DA Admin login page.
  2. Enter your Email or Username (this login accepts either).
  3. Enter your Password.
  4. Optionally check "Remember me", then click Sign In.
Note This admin login is separate from the Superadmin login, which requires an additional authenticator-app code (two-factor authentication) and is used only by the top-level system administrator.

2. Dashboard

Your Dashboard gives you an at-a-glance view of the whole system: total registered farmers, new farmers this month, total data records, and any urgent actions that need your attention. The sidebar is your main way of navigating between Market Prices, Announcements, Inbox, Users, Datasets, Data Import, Crop Monitoring, and Provincial Monitoring.

3. Market Prices

Manage the crop prices that farmers see on their end. You can add new price entries or update existing ones — price per kilogram, demand level, and market location — which farmers will see reflected in their own Market Prices tab.

4. Announcements

Use this to post advisories, weather warnings, or general updates to farmers.

  1. Click "New Announcement."
  2. Fill in the title, message content, type (e.g. general, weather, market, advisory, urgent), and priority.
  3. Optionally target specific municipalities instead of broadcasting province-wide.
  4. Optionally send it as an SMS blast to farmers in the targeted area(s), in addition to it appearing in-app.
  5. Publish it.
It shows up fast As soon as you publish, farmers see a floating notification pop up on their screen within a few seconds — no matter which page they're on.

5. Inbox / Messages

This works the same way as the farmer's Inbox, but from your side: view every conversation with farmers, reply directly, use Quick Replies for common responses, and start new conversations with a specific farmer using the "New" button. An unread-message badge on the sidebar lets you know when a farmer is waiting for a reply.

6. Users Management

Open Users to manage every account in the system — farmers, DA Admins, and Admins.

You'll see stat cards (Total Users, Farmers, DA Admins, Active Users) and a searchable, filterable table of all accounts with their role, location, and status.

Adding a user

Click "Add User" and provide: Full Name, Email, Password, RSBSA Number, Role (Farmer or DA Admin), and Municipality.

Importing many farmers at once

Click "Import Users" and upload a CSV or Excel file. Make sure it includes an RSBSA Number column for each farmer — this is what allows them to use passwordless RSBSA login afterward.

Editing and removing accounts

  • Edit updates a user's name, email, RSBSA number, phone, location, or status.
  • Archive/Restore and Delete are available for farmer accounts.
  • "Delete All Farmers" is a bulk action — use with care, as it removes every farmer account.
Heads up As a DA Officer, new accounts you create start with "Pending" status and need approval. Full Admins can set any status directly.

7. Datasets

This is your library of the data already loaded into SmartHarvest — Crop Production Statistics, Climate & Weather Data, Market Prices, and Livestock & Poultry Inventory. Each dataset card shows how many records it has and when it was last updated. Use View to preview the records, Export to download them, or Delete to remove a dataset entirely.

Note This page is for browsing data that's already in the system. To bring in new data, use Data Import instead.

8. Data Import

Use this page to upload new data into SmartHarvest via CSV file.

  1. Choose a Dataset Type from the dropdown.
  2. Click "Download CSV Template" to get a correctly formatted blank file for that type.
  3. Fill in the template with your data.
  4. Drag-and-drop the completed CSV file (or click to browse), then click "Upload Dataset."
Dataset TypeRequired Columns
Crop Production Statistics (Monthly)Municipality, Farm_Type, Year, Month, Crop, Area_Planted_Ha, Area_Harvested_Ha, Production_MT, Productivity_MT_Ha
Climate & Weather DataYear, Month, Municipality, Rainfall, Avg_Temperature, Humidity
Agricultural Market PricesDate, Crop_Name, Price_Per_Kg, Market_Location, Demand_Level
Livestock & Poultry Inventory (Bi-annual)Year, Period, Municipality, Animal_Type, Headcount, Farm_Type

A Recent Uploads table at the bottom keeps a log of past imports and their status.

9. Crop Monitoring

This gives you a province-wide view of everything farmers have entered in their own "My Crops" section.

  • Stat cards: Total Farmers, Total Crop Records, Total Area, Expected Yield.
  • Charts: Crop Production by Type, Crop Status Distribution, and Production by Municipality.
  • A detailed, searchable table of every farmer-submitted crop record — filterable by status, location, and harvest month.

Click Refresh any time to pull the latest data.

10. Provincial Monitoring

A climate and weather risk dashboard covering all 13 municipalities in Benguet.

  • Stat cards: Watch Status Areas, Municipalities Monitored, Favorable, Under Watch.
  • Pick a municipality to see its current hazard alerts (if any) and a 7-day rainfall forecast.
  • A grid of all 13 municipalities shows each one's status (Favorable, Normal, or Watch) along with live rainfall, temperature, and humidity readings.

This page auto-refreshes every 5 minutes, or you can refresh manually.

11. Language & Logout

Use the language dropdown in the top bar to change the interface language. To sign out, use the Logout option in the top-right corner.
